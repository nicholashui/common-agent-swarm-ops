# Collaboration — step-by-step user guide

**Screen:** Collaboration  
**Route:** `/collaboration`  
**Who it’s for:** Operators reviewing shared items and co-edit queues.

---

## 1. Open Collaboration

1. Sign in → **GOVERNANCE** → **Collaboration**, or `/collaboration`.
2. Confirm shared list, tabs, and share UI when projected.

---

## 2. Browse shared items

1. Search/filter the collaboration list locally if controls exist.
2. Open an item to view summary and CTAs.

---

## 3. Share flow

1. Click **Share** (or primary share control).
2. Share modal may open; permissions remain server-controlled.
3. **Copy link** is local-preview messaging; access is host-revocable.
4. Closing the modal returns to the list.

---

## 4. Item actions

1. CTAs that imply merge/eval require authorized stages (fail-closed).
2. Do not invent collaborator ACLs in the browser.

---

## 5. Help

1. Help → this guide.
2. Collaboration is non-authority presentation plus host-gated actions.
